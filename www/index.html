New damage rules
Drag anywhere on the screen to move your army, in any direction. It can go partway up the road to dodge; let go and it slides back down. Everyone shoots on their own. Shoot a gate to pump its number.
Every gate adds one kind of soldier. Strong kinds come in smaller numbers.
Take a kind's gate three times and the next row is its upgrade: two gold gates, pick one. Six times, and that upgrade gets its own pair. Soldiers are expendable: every hit takes the same share of each kind, however big your army (a brute takes a fifth). Shields soak area hits first. Don't just dodge: enemies that get past you fill the BREACH bar, and a full bar loses the run.
Building pays. Every gate of a kind adds a pip: +10% damage, up to 10 pips. FOCUS adds your share of a kind on top, so an army that is all one kind does double damage with it.
RALLY: the crowd plants and fires three times as fast for 2 s. Auto Rally fires it for you; turn it off below to tap the button yourself (or touch anywhere with a second finger). Fire boosts add together, up to four times the fire. Shoot open cages to free soldiers. Touch a supply drop to use it. Pink stripes mean danger: get out before they fill. After the Tank, the road forks: pick your way to the last boss.
Hold enough of one kind plus its Officer and a gold FORGE gate appears. Walk through it to fuse them into one big unit. Some cards evolve when you reach the right upgrade.
